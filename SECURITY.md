## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take security issues strictly. If you find any vulnerability in our projects, please follow the steps below to report it.

### Do's
*   **DO** Report the vulnerability via email to `security@example.com` (Replace with actual email).
*   **DO** Include clear steps to reproduce the issue.
*   **DO** Wait for our team to acknowledge the receipt of your report.

### Don'ts
*   **DO NOT** Open a public GitHub Issue for security vulnerabilities.
*   **DO NOT** Disclose the vulnerability publicly until it has been patched.

## Response Timeline
*   We aim to acknowledge all reports within 48 hours.
*   We will provide a timeline for the fix after the initial assessment.

Thank you for helping keep Circle secure!
